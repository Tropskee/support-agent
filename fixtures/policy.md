# Czeropski Grading customer policy

## 1. How we handle support
<!-- keywords: -->
We answer every ticket within one business day. We never share order details with anyone but the account holder, identified by the account email. We never change a grade, confirm authenticity, or promise money in a support reply; those go through the processes below.

## 2. Service tiers and turnaround
<!-- keywords: turnaround, how long, tier, express, standard, value, premium, when will, eta -->
Value $25/card, declared value up to $500, 45 business days. Standard $50/card, up to $1,500, 20 business days. Express $150/card, up to $5,000, 5 business days. Premium $300/card, up to $25,000, 2 business days. Turnaround starts the day we receive the package.

## 3. Declared value and upcharges
<!-- keywords: declared, upcharge, extra, charged more, adjustment, invoice, value -->
If a card's market value at its assigned grade exceeds the tier's declared value cap, we move it to the tier that covers it and charge the difference in tier fees as a "Declared value adjustment". Customers can dispute an adjustment within 30 days; a dispute is reviewed against our valuation table.

## 4. Grades and regrades
<!-- keywords: grade, regrade, dispute, why did, only got, corners, edges, centering, surface, re-grade -->
4.1 Every graded card has a defect report listing corner, edge, surface and centering findings. 4.2 A regrade request costs $25 per card and is refunded if the grade goes up. The card must be sent back in its slab, uncracked. 4.3 Support staff cannot change a grade; only a regrade can.

## 5. Order status and delays
<!-- keywords: status, where is, update, late, delay, promised, still, waiting, taking -->
Status moves received → grading → shipped → delivered. If an order passes its promised date, we refund the difference to the next slower tier on request, or the full tier fee if it is more than 10 business days late.

## 6. Shipping, packing and returns
<!-- keywords: ship, packing, pack, sleeve, toploader, box, address, tracking, return -->
Pack each card in a penny sleeve and a semi-rigid holder, sandwiched between cardboard. Return shipping is $20 per order, insured when declared value exceeds $500. The return address can be changed only before the order ships.

## 7. Loss and damage
<!-- keywords: lost, missing, empty, damaged, broken, cracked, crushed, never arrived, stolen -->
7.1 Report loss or damage within 5 days of delivery, with photos of the packaging and contents. 7.2 For insured returns we open a carrier claim and pay out declared value once the claim closes; we do not replace cards. 7.3 Every loss or damage report is reviewed by a person.

## 8. Refunds and billing
<!-- keywords: refund, charged twice, double, charge, billing, money back, chargeback, dispute with bank -->
Duplicate charges are refunded in full. Fees for completed grading are not refundable except under 5 and 8. Chargeback or legal threats go to a person.

## 9. Label corrections
<!-- keywords: label, typo, wrong name, misspelled, slab says, wrong card name -->
If a slab label has an error we made, we reprint it free: the customer sends the slab back with a prepaid label we email.

## 10. Authenticity and cert verification
<!-- keywords: fake, counterfeit, cert, verify, authentic, real, bought, registry -->
Anyone can verify a cert number. If a slab's card doesn't match our registry, treat the slab as suspect. We ask for photos and escalate to a person; support never confirms or denies authenticity in a reply.

## 11. Accepted cards
<!-- keywords: accept, japanese, sealed, oversized, jumbo, energy, do you grade, can i send -->
We grade English and Japanese Pokemon cards of standard size, including promos. We do not grade oversized cards, sealed product or cards in cases we can't open.

## 12. Accounts and privacy
<!-- keywords: account, password, email, login, delete, privacy, change email -->
Account changes are made from the account email only. We can resend a password reset link. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days once orders are closed.
